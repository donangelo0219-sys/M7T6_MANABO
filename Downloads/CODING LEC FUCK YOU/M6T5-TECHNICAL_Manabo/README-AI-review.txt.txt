**Features & Functionality**

* What happens when a user submits the contact form (does it show a simulated confirmation, send via a third-party service, or clear the inputs)?
When the form is submitted with valid information, it displays “Your form is valid” but does not send the message to a real service.

* Which specific visitor settings are saved in `localStorage` (e.g., dark mode preference, form drafts, scent preferences)?
The website saves the dark-mode preference, visitor name, and last-visit date in localStorage.

* What specific validation rules are enforced on the contact form?
The form requires a name, a valid email address, and a message, while the phone number is optional.
